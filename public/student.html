A college-essay workbook

Map the arc before you write the essay.

  1. Go small and specific. One moment you could film says more than a summary of four years.
  2. Show, then tell. A few sentences of scene, a sentence or two of what it meant. Alternate.
  3. Spend your words on the response. The challenge is the setup; what you did and learned is the essay.
